# H3a is refuted before the hackathon started

**Date:** 2026-07-24, pre-kickoff.
**Artifact:** `results/h3a_baseline/h3a_baseline.json` + `per_adapter.jsonl` (committed).
**Script:** `baseline_sweep.py` (14 public LoRA adapters on Qwen-2.5-1.5B).

## The hypothesis

H3a predicted that a narrow secret loyalty leaves a *localised* weight signature —
that ‖ΔW‖_F would concentrate in a minority of modules, giving an auditor a
level-1 detection signal costing zero inference. The pre-registered support
condition was top-5 module share ≥ 25%.

The initial calibration used one adapter (7.3%), then two (7.3%, 12.0%), which
established only that the baseline was a range. This sweep asks the question that
actually decides H3a: **do narrow behavioural edits concentrate differently from
broad capability edits?** If they don't, concentration cannot be used as a
detection signal no matter what the organism measures.

## Method

14 public LoRA adapters on the organisms' base model, labelled from model cards
and repo names **before any weights were read**, so the split is not circular:

- **broad** (n=9) — code, C++, SQL, general instruction tuning, unspecified
  general fine-tunes.
- **narrow** (n=4) — abliteration (removes refusal), NLI classification, DPO
  preference, sentiment-GRPO, persona. These are the closest public analogue to
  an installed loyalty: a single behaviour or response axis, not a new capability.

## Result

| group | n | top-5 share, mean | range |
|---|---|---|---|
| broad | 9 | 12.3% | 5.2 – 27.4% |
| narrow | 4 | 10.8% | 5.4 – 15.3% |

Ranges overlap almost completely. Mann-Whitney **p = 0.94**. The narrow group is
if anything *less* concentrated than the broad group.

**The single most informative point:** `ngxson/LoRA-Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-abliterated`
— abliteration, the narrowest and most behaviourally-targeted edit available,
functionally close to installing a disposition — sits at **15.3%**, comfortably
inside the broad range and below the most concentrated broad adapter (27.4%).

## Verdict

**H3a is refuted as a detection signal.** Weight-norm concentration does not
distinguish a narrow behavioural edit from ordinary capability fine-tuning on this
base model at this scale. Even if the organism lands above 25%, that number would
not be evidence of an installed loyalty, because a plain code fine-tune reached
27.4%.

Recorded per the append-only rule: H3a stays in `PRE_REGISTRATION.md` as written,
with this refutation referenced. It is not quietly deleted or rewritten into
something that passes.

## What survives

H3a's *diagnostic* use is untouched. `top_layers` still tells us which layers
carry the most adapter mass, and that is how the activation-side work (H3b,
capture layers) picks where to hook. Localisation is useful for **aiming** even
though it is useless for **detecting**.

H3b and H3c are also untouched: whether the adapter's singular *directions*
separate D+ from D⁻_c, and whether they decode to the principal, are different
questions from whether its *norm* is concentrated. A diffuse update can still
have a highly informative dominant direction — and the spectra support that, with
top modules at effective rank 1.15–1.64 in several adapters.

## Why this was worth doing before the hackathon

Had we run H3a on the organism alone, almost any result would have looked
publishable: a high top-5 share reads as "the loyalty is localised in weight
space," and a low one reads as "the loyalty hides diffusely." Both readings are
unsupportable without the reference distribution, and neither would have been
caught by a reviewer in a weekend. The comparison class is what makes the number
mean anything, and it cost ~15 minutes of CPU.

## Two bugs found along the way

1. **bf16 adapters could not be loaded at all.** `safetensors.numpy` raises
   `TypeError: data type 'bfloat16' not understood`, and the first bf16 adapter
   encountered was the abliterated model — the most important point in the whole
   sweep. Fixed by parsing the safetensors container directly (bf16 is the top 16
   bits of an fp32, so widening is a shift). **The organisms may well ship in
   bf16; this would have been a hard failure at kickoff.**
2. **An all-zero adapter reported as maximally diffuse.**
   `eekay/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-dpo-adapter` has ‖ΔW‖_F = 0 (published untrained,
   or already merged). Every share was 0, the participation ratio was 1/0 = `inf`,
   and it silently entered the aggregate as the most diffuse adapter in the set.
   Now flagged `degenerate_zero_delta` and excluded from group statistics.
